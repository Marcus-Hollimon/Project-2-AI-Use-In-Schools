Is It Cheating?

Does Berry have a policy on using generative AI?
Berry College does not currently have a comprehensive policy on the use of generative AI or LLMs. However, every instructor is required to have a statement in their syllabus regarding whether or not generative AI can be used in their classroom and if so, how these tools may be used on assignments. If you do want to use generative AI on an assignment it is highly recommended that you ask your instructor and be completely transparent about its use.

The best time to talk with your instructor is before you begin your assignment to avoid needing to start over if generative AI isn't allowed, or if you may have used it in an unprescribed manner.

Be specific in how you plan to use generative AI. Would you like to use ChatGPT or Copilot to help brainstorm ideas or come up with a topic for your assignment? Are you using it to summarize or explain complex concepts? Or do you plan to use it for writing and editing? Be prepared that your instructor may approve some use cases but not others.

Have a plan for giving credit. APA Style, MLA Style, and Chicago Style all have guidelines for citing generative AI. Your instructor may also ask for an appendix that includes the prompts that you provided to the generative AI or the full transcript of your interaction.

What is Academic Integrity?

Academic integrity refers to maintaining a standard of honest and ethical behavior in all types of academic work. This includes things like not cheating on exams or turning in a term paper that you didn't write yourself. It also includes plagiarism, or not giving proper credit to other people's ideas or work. All students are subject to Berry College's code for academic integrity. A link to Berry's academic integrity policy is below.

Academic integrity and its relation to generative AI: Please refrain from presenting AI-generated material as your own original work. While certain assignments might allow you to leverage AI to enhance your creative capabilities, it should not replace genuine creative thinking, especially in an educational setting. Whenever you incorporate content generated by AI, remember to include proper citations. This way, instructors can easily trace the sources of the AI-generated content. Transparency is an imperative for using gen AI ethically and with academic integrity.
