Resources

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools and Sample Syllabus Policy Statements is a webpage from the Office of the Provost that provides information and recommendations about the use of AI for learning.

Library Student Guide to ChatGPT from UMKC University Libraries is a resource for students on how to ethically use ChatGPT and other AI programs, including citing the use of AI and verifying information given by generative AI.

The Center of Excellence: AI Powered Spatial Computing has a mission to provide underrepresented minority students spatial computing skill-building experiences at a professionally proficient level to contribute to the growth of the advancing technology workforce in the Midwest, including an objective to build AI-empowered learning environments.

AI Tools is a webpage from Information Services that offers license access to AI productivity tools — such as ChatGPT and Microsoft 365 Copilot — with guidelines specifying acceptable data classification levels and data security requirements. This page also provides information on other AI tools for faculty, as well as training resources.
