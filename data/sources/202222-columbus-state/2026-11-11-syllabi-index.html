Syllabi

The College posts course-level syllabi to support compliance with Ohio Revised Code 3345.029 and to provide public access to course information.

The syllabi listed below are organized alphabetically by course subject, or alpha prefix, and then numerically by course number. A review of posted syllabi is conducted on a term-by-term basis.

Section-level syllabi are maintained by the academic departments. Additionally, the College maintains Standard Syllabus Statements and Consumer Information - Student Right to Know through the linked resources. 

For more information, or to request a copy of a syllabus, please contact the Registrar at registrar@cscc.edu.
